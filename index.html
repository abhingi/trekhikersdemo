Your Dehradun-Based Himalayan Travel Partner

Trekhickers organizes practical, economical, and memorable mountain expeditions across Uttarakhand and Himachal Pradesh. Whether you are planning a winter summit like Kedarkantha, an alpine lake trek to Chandranahan, or a spiritual Do Dham or Chardham Yatra, our packages bundle housing, meals, activities, and transport so you never have to worry about the logistics.

Our team pairs well-educated local trek leaders with dependable mountain transport and 24×7 client support—earning trust across Uttarakhand for safety, hospitality, and transparent pricing.
